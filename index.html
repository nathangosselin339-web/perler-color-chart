Notes & sources

  • SKUs are the official 1,000-bead bag numbers (80-XXXXX) sold by Perler; colors marked mix only are not sold as single-color bags.
  • P-numbers follow the community-standard numbering of Perler's printed color chart. For the 2021 additions the P-number order does not match SKU order (verified against perler.com product listings).
  • Colors marked new have not yet been assigned chart numbers: the 2022–2024 additions (Celery, Sunflower, Lemon, Neon Blue, Coral, Brick, Rich Butter, Peacock, Carnation Pink) and the 2026 U.S. releases from the Vivid Luxe, Secret Garden and Not So Basics collections (Toffee–Lipstick).
  • Hex values for the 2026 colors were sampled from Perler's official product swatch photos (PER17646–PER17668).
  • Solid-color hex values are screen approximations from photographed beads (Perlervault / beadcolors dataset). Values marked approx are estimates for specialty finishes (pearl, neon, glow, glitter, metallic) which shift under light.
  • Striped beads contain multiple colors and have no single hex; swatches are representative.
  • Data compiled from perler.com, github.com/maxcleme/beadcolors, pixel-pattern.com, pixel-beads.com and fusebeadideas.com.
